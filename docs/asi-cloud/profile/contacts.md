title: Contacts
id: contacts
---

If you add an email address to the contacts section of your profile, you will be able to receive important notifications about your account.
Each contact address that you add can be assigned three different roles:
- **Service**: to receive notifications about start/stop VM actions.
- **Financial**: to receive low balance alerts.
- **Marketing**: to receive marketing updates and newsletters.
