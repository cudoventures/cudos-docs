The identities are the login methods into your account.
You can add as many identities into your ASI:Cloud account as you want.

![profile_identities](@site/static/img/identities.png)

## Blockchain addresses for payments and discounts

In order to top up your ASI:Cloud balance, you will need to connect at least one blockchain identity.
That is because payments from both the payments page and Squid Router can only be performed from a blockchain account that is connected to your ASI:Cloud account as an identity.

Moreover, the [discounts for staked FET](/docs/asi-cloud/discounts-and-bonuses) are calculated with the sum of the stake of all the ASI accounts that are connected to your ASI:Cloud account as identities.
